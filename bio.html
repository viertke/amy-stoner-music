Amy Friedl Stoner

Amy Friedl Stoner is an accomplished and versatile cabaret/jazz singer born and raised in Cedar Rapids, Iowa. Amy made her Carnegie Hall debut in May 2018 as a featured performer in "All the Way: The Music of Jimmy Van Heusen" produced by the Mabel Mercer Foundation.

Her solo cabaret shows have sold out in venues around the US, including The Gaslight Theatre (St. Louis), Davenport's (Chicago), and Cabaret in the Courtyard (Cedar Rapids). Amy has also been warmly received in other notable venues such as the famous Birdland Jazz Club (NYC), Don't Tell Mama (NYC), Kranzburg Arts Center (St. Louis), and Noce (Des Moines). In March 2017, she debuted her show Dreamchaser to rave reviews at the famed Metropolitan Room (NYC).

She has performed with the legendary Marilyn Maye as a special guest in her show at The Inn (Lake Okoboji, IA) and opened for her at the Sheldon Concert Hall (St. Louis). Amy has worked with numerous Tony and Grammy award-winning artists, such as Faith Prince, Christine Ebersole, Jason Robert Brown, and Billy Stritch, music director for Liza Minnelli and Tony Bennett.
