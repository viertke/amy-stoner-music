FAQ Section

HAVE QUESTIONS?

What is the deadline for sponsorship commitment?
Commitments are requested by November 20th, 2025 to ensure inclusion in all print materials, especially the full-color Playbill. Commitments made after this date may be limited to digital recognition only.
Can we customize a sponsorship package?
Absolutely. Our Custom Partnership tier is designed for tailored deliverables. We are happy to work with you to create a package that meets your specific marketing and community engagement goals. Please use the 'Reserve Your Sponsorship Now' button below to start a conversation.
What are "group tickets" and how do we receive them?
Group tickets are discounted blocks of tickets provided as part of your package. You can distribute these to clients, employees, or use them for internal events. Following your sponsorship confirmation, our team will reach out directly to coordinate your specific ticket needs.
How do I submit my materials?
Once your commitment is confirmed, our team will reach out to you directly with a detailed email specifying the required file formats (e.g., logo resolution, ad dimensions) and a secure upload link. Please ensure all materials are submitted by the November 20th, 2025 deadline for inclusion in print materials.
